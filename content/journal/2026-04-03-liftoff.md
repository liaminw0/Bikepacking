Today, after many months of waiting and lots of preparations I finally took my bike and me out for the most questionable adventure I have ever had.

Before leaving completely though, I had the chance to say goodby to some of my friends and family with a nice cup of coffee.
![2685](https://cloud.inwards.io/public.php/dav/files/9pDQditxdfqww9C/2685.webp)

The day has had little trouble. No rain, a nice scenic route and a good place to stay. However, one issue arose halfway through tbe day that has slaughtered my legs... WIND. At some point going anything faster than 10km/h was completly undoable.

![2691](https://cloud.inwards.io/public.php/dav/files/9pDQditxdfqww9C/2691.webp)

At around 5PM I got to the campsite and have been chilling ever since.

![2686](https://cloud.inwards.io/public.php/dav/files/9pDQditxdfqww9C/2686.webp)

I'll be writing on here about once a week. Pictures will be posted more often.
